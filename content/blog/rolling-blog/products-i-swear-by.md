MacBook Pro

This is easily the best laptop by miles. I used macbook pro during work for three years without a problem. I came back to windows machines (Surface book and Lenovo thinkpad) for five years. There was a day and night differecne. If you can afford to invest in a macbook, just for it. It is worth every single penny. I don’t know if I would say the same about the differences in iphone and an android phone, but in the laptop space, the difference is very clear.
