I put together this slide deck for the second guest lecture in my AI series for business students. The first lecture explored [what AI can do, from chat to agents](/blog/what-ai-can-do-from-chat-to-agents/). This one asks how humans stay in charge as AI becomes more capable. No programming background is assumed.

<p><a href="/slides/smarter-than-you-think/" style="display:inline-block;padding:12px 22px;border-radius:8px;background:var(--accent);color:#fff;text-decoration:none;font-weight:600;box-shadow:none;">Open the slide deck →</a></p>

The slides cover:

- What AI can do today, including longer tasks, browser use, and falling costs.
- How language models work, with next-word prediction and attention exercises.
- Where AI struggles: unreliable answers, generic writing, hidden instructions, and agents that work around the rules.
- How context, judgment, and responsibility help people stay in charge.

The deck includes polls, interactive examples, and a coffee-shop activity about deciding which tasks to delegate to AI and which need human oversight.
