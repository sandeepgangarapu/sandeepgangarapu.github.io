1. Get a typical cereal bowl
    
    ![Untitled](/images/recipes/maggi-microwave/untitled.png)
    
2. Put maggi in it
3. Put maggi masala on top
4. Pour water on masala until the water line is in line with maggi surface. This will give medium consistency (soupy + dry)
    1. Below the maggi surface - Dried consistency
    2. Above the maggi surface - Soupy consistency
    3. Adjust the levels as all bowls and microwaves are not the same
5. Put it in microwave and set the timer on default mode for 2 minutes
6. Stir and mix and make sure the surface maggi is now down and vice versa
7. Put it in microwave again and set the timer on default mode for 2 minutes
8. The bowl will be very hot now. Wait a couple minutes and not more (maggi will get soggy). Use a cloth to remove bowl from microwave 
9. Enjoy :)
