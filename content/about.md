I am a Data Scientist at Apple working on Siri, with a background in statistics, machine learning, causal inference, and experimentation.

I completed my PhD in Information and Decision Sciences at the University of Minnesota's Carlson School of Management. Before moving to Minneapolis, I worked at Google as an Automation Specialist on the Google Search Anti-Abuse team, developing scalable solutions to fight abuse on Search. I earned my master's and bachelor's degrees in Aerospace Engineering, specializing in Computational Fluid Dynamics, at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur.

I am from [Kalyanadurgam](https://goo.gl/maps/CJgEUE9FbMT2), a town in the Anantapur district of Andhra Pradesh, India. I love [running](https://www.millcityrunning.com/), snowboarding, and floating on my [Onewheel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XNqOU4jx62I).

## Teaching & speaking

I enjoy sharing ideas about AI and experimentation. My 2026 AI lecture series for business students covers [what AI can do, from chat to agents](/blog/what-ai-can-do-from-chat-to-agents/) and [how humans stay in charge as AI becomes more capable](/blog/smarter-than-you-think-weaker-than-it-looks/). I also gave guest lectures on experimentation at Carnegie Mellon University in 2024 and the University of Texas at Austin in 2023. More talks and milestones are in the [activity archive](/activity-stream/), and student feedback is collected on the [testimonials page](/blog/testimonials/).
