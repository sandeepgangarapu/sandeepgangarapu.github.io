### Corona Daily Updates

[https://coronadailyupdates.org/](https://coronadailyupdates.org/). - Deprecated

This service sends SMS to users in India with COVID-19 numbers in their district, state, country. The main motivation is to serve a large demographic of under-served people who are not digitally savvy to get this information from dashboards/chatbots.
