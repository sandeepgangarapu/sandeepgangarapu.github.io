## Garden Genie

An app for keeping track of plants and garden tasks, with care details for watering, sunlight, soil, and harvests.

[iOS app on GitHub](https://github.com/sandeepgangarapu/gardengenie-ios)

## Snow Scanner

Snowfall alerts for the ski resorts you care about. Set a snowfall threshold and get an email when the snow meets it.

[snowscanner.app](https://snowscanner.app/)

## This Day Last Year

A [TRMNL](https://trmnl.com/) recipe that compares today's forecast with the same date last year. It puts high and low temperatures and rainfall side by side on an e-ink display, using Open-Meteo weather data.

## AI Brief

A daily email with short summaries from the AI writers I follow. My Mac reads their RSS feeds and sends the brief around 7 AM Pacific.

[Read AI Brief or subscribe](https://ainews.sandeepgangarapu.com/)

## Poll Normalizer bot

A past bot project that normalized poll results when “Show me the results” was the last option, removing those votes and recalculating the remaining percentages. Its [Twitter profile](https://twitter.com/poll_normalizer) documents the project.
